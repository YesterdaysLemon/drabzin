Drabzin Vectorizer

1Image

Each image opens in its own tab. Your work on each one is saved in this browser.

2Crop

Click Find the design, then click a plain spot near the middle of the panel, paper or board. The 4 corners jump to its edges.

Or click Move corners and drag the 4 dots yourself. Leave out text and measurements. Photo taken at an angle? Put the dots on the real corners of the panel. The app makes it straight.

3Real size

How big is the real design? Enter its width or its height.

4Type of design

Makes every line straight and every angle exact.

5Fix the result

Wrong parts cut? Show the app the colours. Click a button, then click that colour in the picture.

Parts missing? Move it left. Background included? Move it right.

Higher makes curves smoother but removes small detail.

More settings

Corners sharper than this stay sharp. Others are rounded.

Arcs make smooth cuts and small files. Choose short lines only if your machine cannot read arcs.

Scroll to zoom. Drag to move.

Turn a picture of a design into a DXF file for cutting.

  1. Choose an image of the design.
  2. Move the 4 corners onto the design.
  3. Enter the real width or height.
  4. Check the red cut lines.
  5. Click Download DXF. It opens in AutoCAD.

Your image stays on your computer. It is never uploaded.
